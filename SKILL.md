# Free100InfluencerLeads

[Free100InfluencerLeads](https://free100influencerleads.com) is a lead generation platform focused on Instagram micro-influencers, drawing from a database of 300,000-plus contacts with follower and category details. It is part of the same free-daily-leads network as [Free100Leads](https://free100leads.com).

## Claiming free influencer leads

Any visitor can claim up to 100 free influencer contacts per day, no signup or credit card required, with the allocation resetting daily.

## Filtering by follower count and creator type

Filters cover follower count range (roughly 100 to 5,000-plus), country, industry, and creator-specific job titles such as Photographer, Digital Creator, Athlete, and Public Figure, which suits campaigns targeting micro-influencers rather than celebrity accounts.
